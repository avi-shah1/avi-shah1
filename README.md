## Projects

- **[HypeCreate](https://hypecreate.vercel.app)**: finds the short videos that are working in a niche right now and turns them into ready-to-post ads for a brand. It scans recent TikTok and Instagram videos from small creators, ranks them by views per follower, has an AI model explain why the winners work, then writes a script in that format and generates the video, checked against the brand's rules. 🥇 Compound Viral Award at Hack the Feed, built with [Ben](https://github.com/benthomasmtl). [Live demo](https://hypecreate.vercel.app).

- **[O-1A Visa Vetting Agent](https://github.com/avi-shah1/o1-visa-vetting-agent)**: a multi-agent system that vets O-1A visa applicants for immigration teams. The applicant fills in an intake form, and five research agents check each claim in parallel against Devpost, LinkedIn, GitHub, OpenAlex and the press. A reconciler then marks every claim as corroborated, partly verified, unverified or contradicted. The system asks the applicant follow-up questions, compares the case with published appeal decisions, and builds a source-linked dossier for an attorney. Next.js, TypeScript, Mastra, Claude, Exa, Apify, SQLite/Drizzle, Python. 🥉 3rd place at the Anything Applied Agentic Hackathon, built with [Saqif](https://github.com/Saqif-Ayaan), who built the scoring agent. [Live demo](https://o1-visa-vetting-agent.vercel.app/case/demo?replay=1) · [Demo video](https://www.loom.com/share/c5d85f5988e8424caace7a379dfa6ac8).

- **[AcquiScreener](https://github.com/avi-shah1/finance-ai-lab)**: a buy-side M&A target screener. You enter an acquirer, a sector and a geography. It finds candidate companies, gathers evidence from company websites, Companies House, SEC EDGAR and the news, and rates each candidate against the buyer on strategic fit, commercial attractiveness, deal feasibility and timing. Every rating must cite its evidence: code turns any rating without a valid citation into "Insufficient Evidence". Python, Flask, PostgreSQL (Supabase), Claude, Exa, deployed on Railway. [Live app](https://finance-ai-lab-production.up.railway.app/) (password protected).

- **[FaceTag](https://github.com/avi-shah1/calhacks-13.0-facetag)**: an AI networking assistant for Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses. It recognises enrolled contacts with on-device face matching and shows their name and background on the lens. It also listens for factual questions during a conversation and starts answering before the speaker has finished. Swift, SwiftUI, Apple Vision, Apple SpeechAnalyzer. Built at Cal Hacks 13.0 with Toby, Joaquin and Mirabelle. [Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/meta-glasses-v2kp8a).

- **[Restaurant Procurement Agent](https://github.com/avi-shah1/restaurant-procurement-agent)**: forecasts what a kitchen is about to run out of, searches the web for better supplier prices, and recommends what to order. Python does all the arithmetic, and the agent only chooses between options Python has already priced. A person approves every decision before a request-for-quote draft is created. Python, Flask, Supabase, ZooWork Managed Agents, Tavily. One-day hackathon build. [Live demo](https://restaurant-procurement-agent-xi.vercel.app/).

- **[Polty](https://github.com/avi-shah1/ghost-personal-agent/tree/polty-hackathon)**: a voice agent with no body of its own that borrows devices with permission (your phone's camera, smart lights, an Arduino, live traffic cameras) and shows what it sees on a live canvas. [Live demo](https://ghost-agents-production-62dd7a4d.up.railway.app).

- **[Tradie Talk](https://github.com/avi-shah1/trades-project)**: practise handling complaint calls as a tradesperson. Pick a scenario, talk out loud to an AI customer, then get a scored debrief. Next.js, TypeScript, ElevenLabs voice agents, Claude. Built at an SF Tech Week hackathon. [Live demo](https://trades-project.vercel.app).
